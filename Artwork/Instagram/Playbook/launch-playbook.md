**Prepared as a full launch campaign: brand foundation → first 9 posts → 30-day calendar → content system → design system → idea banks.**

Everything here is built to be executed by a photographer, designer, AI image generator, copywriter, and social manager without further questions.

## 0. Read This First — Two Strategic Truths

**1. Your real photography is bright, not dark.**
The creative brief described dark, dripping, moody "food-porn." Your actual product photos are the opposite — and better: bright morning light, white speckled ceramic plates, linen, warm wood, a windowsill, the burrito sliced clean to show the cross-section, and chipotle mayo in a little speckled ramekin. That look is *premium-but-homey* — Kinfolk breakfast, not steakhouse. **We are building the entire system around your real look.** Do not chase dark food-porn; it would break your grid on day one.

**2. You have three unfair advantages — lead with them.**

- **Woman-owned.** Not a footnote — a headline. It's trust, story, and community all at once.
- **Sunday-only.** Scarcity is a gift. "You can only get this one morning a week" is a hook most food brands would kill for. It makes every Sunday an event.
- **The chipotle mayo.** Your own site says "people come back for the sauce." That's your signature. Sauce is the hero prop in half your content.

**Positioning line (internal):** *The Sunday breakfast burrito LA plans its weekend around — woman-owned, small-batch, and famous for the sauce.*

**One-line bio (for the profile):**
> Woman-owned Sunday breakfast burritos 🌅 Small-batch, hand-rolled, famous chipotle mayo. Order by Sat 3PM → [link]

## 2. The Grid Strategy — How the First 9 Work Together

A first-time visitor sees all 9 at once before they read a single caption. So the grid has to answer *what, why-different, how-good, who-you-are* in one glance. We use a **cohesion pattern**, not nine random posts:

**Layout logic (checkerboard of "hero food" and "brand/graphic" tiles):**

```
[1 Hero food ]  [2 Purple brand ]  [3 Hero food ]
[4 Sauce close]  [5 Hero food   ]  [6 Purple brand]
[7 Hero food ]  [8 Purple brand ]  [9 Community  ]
```

- **Corners and center are food** (posts 1, 3, 7, 9 corners + 5 center) → the grid reads as "food-first" instantly.
- **Purple graphic tiles (2, 6, 8)** carry the words — what/when/why — so the story is legible even on mute.
- **Alternating light (food) and deep-purple (brand) tiles** create the premium rhythm that makes a new grid look art-directed instead of accidental.

Post the grid **bottom-to-top / in reverse** so that when finished, Post 1 (the hero) sits top-left. Practically: publish Post 9 first, Post 1 last, over a 9–12 day seeding window (see calendar).

**Global art-direction rules for all 9 (the "grid glue"):**
- Food tiles: bright natural window light, white speckled ceramic, linen, warm wood, chipotle mayo ramekin in frame, shallow depth of field, cream/neutral backgrounds.
- Brand tiles: deep purple `#3D1A5C` field, golden-yellow display type, the sunrise arcs in a corner.
- One consistent light direction (from a left/side window) across all food shots.
- Never mix a cool-blue or dark-moody frame into this grid.

## 6. Community & Growth Strategy

### Engagement strategy
- **Reply to every comment within the first 60 minutes** of posting (the algorithm's golden window) and to every DM same-day. Early engagement velocity is everything for a new account.
- **Comment out** as the brand on 15–20 LA food accounts, foodies, and neighbors daily during launch month (`#eaterla`, `#lafoodie`, local food bloggers). Be genuinely funny/warm, never spammy — no "check us out." Presence, not pitching.
- **Ask a question in every caption** so comments have an easy on-ramp ("which one's yours?", "extra sauce — yes or obviously?").

### UGC strategy
- Put a **card in every delivery**: "Post your Sunday and tag @bobsburritos for a chance at a free burrito." Frictionless, physical, repeatable.
- Create a **branded hashtag** — `#BobsSundayClub` — and feature the best post every Monday.
- **Repost UGC to Stories always, to feed weekly** (with permission). Reward tagging publicly so others copy it.
- Offer a tiny incentive: tag us → get a **free chipotle-mayo upgrade** next order. Low cost, high participation.

### Giveaway ideas
1. **"Sunday on us"** — follow + tag 2 friends + save the post → one winner gets next Sunday's order free. (Launch-month growth driver.)
2. **"Bring a neighbor"** — winner + a friend both get a free burrito; doubles reach and referral.
3. **"Name the special"** — fans suggest a limited burrito name; winner's pick gets made and they eat free. (Co-creation = loyalty.)
4. **"Sauce lover"** — comment your wildest thing to put chipotle mayo on; funniest wins a month of free sauce upgrades.
5. **Milestone drops** — at 500/1k followers, free burritos to the first N Sunday orders.

Keep giveaways **simple, local, and tied to ordering** — not generic "win an iPad" bait that attracts non-customers.

### Comment & DM response tone
Warm, quick, a little playful, human. Match the customer's energy, use their name, never copy-paste corporate lines. Emojis okay in moderation (🌅🌯🌶️💛). Turn compliments into soft CTAs ("ahh thank you!! see you Sunday? 🌅"). Handle a complaint fast, publicly acknowledge, move to DM to fix, then over-deliver. Never argue in comments.

## 7. The Design System

Everything below keeps the grid looking like one intentional brand. Hand this section to any designer or photographer and they can match the look exactly.

### 7.1 Photography rules
- **Light:** always bright, soft, natural window light from one side (left preferred). Morning warmth. **Never** hard flash, never dark/moody, never cool-blue.
- **Surfaces:** white speckled ceramic plates, warm wood boards, linen (cream/oatmeal). No bright-colored or patterned plates.
- **The slice:** hero burritos are always cut and standing cross-section to camera. Show the fluff, the melt, the layers.
- **The sauce is always in frame** for food shots — the speckled ramekin is a recurring character.
- **Backgrounds:** cream/neutral walls, soft-blurred windows or plants. Keep it uncluttered.
- **Depth of field:** shallow (f/2.2–f/3.2) for singles, f/4 for the lineup. Sharp on the cross-section.
- **Steam/freshness:** capture gentle steam where possible; it signals "just made."
- **Aspect ratio:** shoot 4:5 (feed) and 9:16 (Stories/Reels). Never post 1:1 crops that waste feed height.
- **Editing:** warm white balance, lifted shadows, natural saturation (don't over-orange the food), slight clarity on textures. Consistent preset across every post — build one Lightroom preset and never deviate.

### 7.2 Typography
- **Display / headlines:** a bold, chunky geometric display face echoing your logo's blocky character — e.g. **Clash Display (Bold)**, **Chubbo**, or **Cal Sans**. All-caps for headlines, tight leading. Golden `#FFD23F` on purple, or purple on cream/yellow.
- **Body / UI:** a clean modern grotesk — **Inter**, **General Sans**, or **Satoshi**. Sentence case, comfortable letter-spacing.
- **Hierarchy:** Headline (chunky display, 80–120px) → Subhead (grotesk medium, 30–40px) → Body/meta (grotesk regular, 24–28px). Never more than two type sizes competing in one tile.
- **Never** use script fonts, thin fashion serifs, or default Canva fonts.

### 7.3 Color usage
- Deep Purple `#3D1A5C` = primary brand field and text-on-light.
- Golden Yellow `#FFD23F` = headlines, CTAs, accents. Your "click here" color.
- Sunrise Orange `#FF8A3D` and Lavender `#C9A7F0` = secondary accents and the sunrise arcs only — never large text.
- Warm Cream `#FBF3E4` = light backgrounds and body text on purple.
- **60/30/10:** ~60% neutral (photo/cream), 30% purple, 10% yellow/orange accent per graphic.

### 7.4 Spacing & layout
- **Safe margins:** 96px on brand tiles, 64px minimum on any text over photos.
- **Grid:** align type to a simple 12-column mental grid; left-align by default.
- **Negative space is a feature** — keep at least one-third of every brand tile empty.

### 7.5 Iconography & graphic elements
- **Signature device:** the concentric sunrise arcs (lavender → orange → yellow) in one corner of brand tiles. This is your visual signature — use it on tiles, Story frames, stickers, and delivery labels.
- **Icons:** simple single-weight line icons in golden yellow only (calendar, sunrise, doorbell, whisk, tortilla). No filled/multicolor icons.
- **Shapes:** rounded pill buttons (golden fill, purple text) matching your website's CTA button. Rounded corners (16–24px radius) everywhere; nothing sharp-cornered.
- **Stickers/labels:** a circular "Bob's Burritos · Sunday Club" stamp for wraps and Story reposts.

### 7.6 Templates (build these once, reuse forever)
1. **Hero Food** — full-bleed photo, optional tiny wordmark bottom-right.
2. **Statement Tile** — purple field, chunky golden headline, cream subline, sunrise arcs.
3. **Menu Tile** — burrito photo + golden name/price pill.
4. **How-To / Steps Tile** — purple, three golden-icon steps.
5. **Quote/UGC Tile** — cream field, purple text, customer quote + golden quotation mark.
6. **Countdown / Urgency Tile** — purple, big golden "ORDERS CLOSE SAT 3PM," countdown.

### 7.7 Motion — animations & transitions (Reels/Stories)
- **Text:** words drop or type in one at a time, ease-out, never spin/bounce cheaply.
- **Transitions:** clean cuts on the beat; whip-pan or match-cut between "raw → cooked" or "wrapped → sliced." No cheesy star-wipes.
- **Signature motion:** the sunrise arcs animate outward (radiating) as an intro/outro sting — your 1-second brand bumper on every Reel.
- **Pacing:** food ASMR is slow and lingering; process reels are fast and rhythmic. Match cut to audio.
- **Captions:** always burn in captions (most watch on mute), golden or cream, bottom third, safe from UI.

### 7.8 Instagram consistency checklist (before every post)
Bright natural light? ✓ · Speckled ceramic + linen? ✓ · Sauce ramekin in frame (food posts)? ✓ · Brand colors only on graphics? ✓ · Chunky display + grotesk pairing? ✓ · One bucket? ✓ · Caption ends in a question or CTA? ✓ · Alt text written? ✓ · 4:5 or 9:16? ✓
