# Bob's Burritos — brand kit

## Positioning

Woman-owned · Sunday-only small-batch breakfast burritos · famous house chipotle mayo · delivered at 1111 Wilshire.

**Internal line:** *The Sunday breakfast burrito LA plans its weekend around — woman-owned, small-batch, and famous for the sauce.*

## Colors

| Role | Hex |
|------|-----|
| Primary / grape | `#3D1A5C` |
| Deep | `#2A1140` |
| Gold / CTA | `#FFD23F` |
| Sunrise orange | `#FF8A3D` |
| Lilac | `#C9A7F0` |
| Cream | `#FFF4DC` / `#FBF3E4` |

## Signature device

Concentric **sunrise arcs/rings** (lavender → orange → gold), as on the circular logo badge.

## Type feel

- Display: chunky rounded bold (logo-like) — gold on purple or purple on cream  
- Body: clean grotesk (Fredoka on web)  
- Never thin fashion serifs or random Canva script fonts  

## Voice

Friendly · funny · bold · never corporate. Woman-owned is a **headline**, not a footnote.
