## Origin Story / The Name

**There is no Bob.** The founder is **Khushbu**.

Her name gets misspelled and mispronounced constantly — Cushbob, Kushbu, Cash-bab, Koshbob, and dozens more — on coffee cups, order tickets, everywhere. Rather than fight it, she embraced the most common mangling and named the business **Bob's Burritos**.

So "Bob" is an affectionate nod to a decade of butchered name tags. It quietly reinforces that the brand is **woman-owned** and run by a **real person** who loves breakfast.

**Reveal (use as emotional payoff, not a footnote):**  
*There is no Bob — it's Khushbu.* Then the coffee-cup story. Warm, self-aware, funny. Never corporate origin-myth language.

**Do not invent:** details about when she started cooking, childhood, or other biography. Mark gaps as `[PLACEHOLDER]`.

## Founder / "Meet Bob" (content framing)

| Surface | How to play it |
|---------|----------------|
| **Hook** | "Meet Bob." / "Who's Bob?" |
| **Payoff** | There is no Bob — it's Khushbu. The name is a love letter to every wrong name tag. |
| **Proof** | Real kitchen presence, real burritos, woman-owned Sunday batch |
| **Avoid** | Pretending a man named Bob owns the kitchen; burying the reveal in fine print |

Post 8 and any "Meet Bob" tile should land the reveal as the **heart** of the story. Graphic backup is fine until a real founder photo is ready.

**Suggested caption spine (adapt, don't invent bio):**
> You've been wondering who Bob is. Plot twist: there isn't one. I'm Khushbu — and after a decade of coffee cups that said *Cushbob*, *Koshbob*, and things we can't print, I named the kitchen after the mess. Woman-owned. Hand-rolled. Famous for the sauce. Welcome to Bob's. 🌅

## Content thread: "Who's Bob?"

Recurring Story / highlight series. Self-aware, warm, funny, human.

| Format | Idea |
|--------|------|
| **Story highlight** | **Who's Bob?** — pin permanent highlight |
| **Collection** | Real-world misspellings of *Khushbu* (coffee cups, tickets, texts) — with permission when faces appear |
| **Gag still** | Close-up of a coffee cup (or order ticket) with a wrong name |
| **Reel** | Khushbu reading the worst misspellings aloud |
| **CTA** | "Drop the worst spelling you've ever gotten" / "Comment your coffee-cup name" |

Cadence: light and ongoing (not every feed post) — Stories weekly-ish; one strong Reel when you have a pile of tags.

## Voice

Friendly · funny · bold · fresh · premium-but-approachable · **self-aware** · human · **never corporate**.

Talk like a cool friend who makes an incredible burrito — warm, a little cheeky, not salesy.  
The name story unlocks humor and vulnerability without apology. Woman-owned is a **headline**, not a footnote.
